Slow Grow month #2 750 point Battle Report

WHFB6th - month 2 - posted 9th November 2025

Armies

Moving on with our slow grow which started last month at 500 points, the October limit was 750 points. Plenty of choice for both of us for 250 points but at such an early stage we are both still building up the core really. (No Stegadon yet, to Matt's relief).

Matt's Vampire Counts

A reminder of what Matt already had for his initial 500 points;

  • Zviko level 2 Necromancer - Black Periapt
  • Klaus level 1 Necromancer - Spell familiar
  • 10 zombies - banner and musician
  • 10 zombies #2 - banner and musician
  • 10 skeletons - full command

For the additional 250 points this month he chose;

  • +1 level for Klaus the Necromancer and a dispel scroll
  • +1 Wight Lord - Sword of the Kings
  • +3 Fel Bats
  • +2 zombies

Spells

Klaus got the spells: 1. Invocation of Nehek, 4. Gaze of Nagash and 5. Vanhel's Danse Macabre.

Zviko got the spells: 1. Invocation of Nehek, 3. Hellish Vigour

James' Lizardmen

Initial 500 pints;

  • Kai Gor Zlat, Saurus Scar Veteran - Enchanted shield
  • Itza Otltza, skink priest - Mark of the Old Ones, Diadem of power
  • 10 saurus warriors - banner and musician
  • 10 skink skirmishers - javelin and shield

Furnishing my army with another 250 points for this month;

  • +4 saurus warriors, including champion
  • +5 saurus cavalry, banner and champion

Spells

Itza Otltza got the spells: 1. Second Sign of Amul and 3. Forked Lightning

With his Mark of the Old Ones, Itza also rolled for 3 rerolls during the game.

Battlefield & Scenario

For the location of the battle we randomly selected the Badlands, Dark Lands, Border Princes and similar wilderness regions table in the main rulebook. We then each rolled 3 pieces of terrain from the table and ended with;

  • 2 hills
  • 1 shallow river
  • 1 scrub (difficult ground, cover)
  • 1 rocky ridge (difficult ground, some sides may be sheer cliffs)
  • 1 boulders (difficult ground, hard cover)

We both agreed that the terrain probably wouldn't have a big impact on the game, especially the river in the corner. (Spoiler: it did have some impact).

From a couple of tournament packs we have accumulated we randomised the Carnage scenario which, to summarise, rewards winning combat with tokens and chops alternating 18" sections from the end of each player's deployment zone.

Deployment

I won the roll-off to place terrain first and so Matt chose who deployed first. He allowed Lizardmen to place first, realising I would be getting the +1 anyway, being so outnumbered.

I chose to put down my 14 saurus warriors (full command) in a central position, Matt placed his bats on his right flank behind a hill.

Next I placed my skink skirmishers as far right as I could, hoping to get the jump on the bats with javelins before they could become too much of a nuisance. Matt placed his first zombies mirroring my saurus.

My last unit was 5 saurus cavalry (banner & champion) who I placed back slightly from my saurus warriors, aiming to use both units in tandem and hopefully negate some ranks and outnumbering.

Matt placed his skeleton unit in the centre and his other zombie unit on his left flank, ready to move through a gap between the boulders and the rocky ridge.

My skink priest, Itza Otltza, hid within the skirmishers (I didn't want him to become bat food) and Kai Gor Zlat, my scar veteran, joined the saurus warriors. Matt's Wight lord joined the skeletons, Zviko joined the central zombie unit while Klaus joined the flanking zombies.

Turn 1

As with last month, both armies just shuffled towards each other. My skinks headed for the hill hoping to get a vantage for throwing javelins and casting some lightning but also to get some cover from roving bats. Both saurus units just rolled forward.

The undead horde shambled on with the Klaus and the flanking zombies moving for the gap. Matt added some zombies to a unit with irresistible force.

Turn 2

Realising that I needed to get up the field and try to smash through the lines before the already outnumbering corpses got even more numerous, I moved everything up. I was still at this point hoping to somehow get a flank with the saurus cavalry and the warriors in the front. The skinks moved up onto the hill, javelins at the ready for turn 3. I reserved power dice to try and hold back the raises.

Matt largely held his line but Zviko managed to bolster his zombie unit by 11 zombies with another irresistible force spell! Klaus led his zombies into the gap, securing the flank. The bats readied to sandwhich the skinks with some newly raised zombies on the hill.

Turn 3

Going in to turn 3 I was seriously concerned with the number of foes that kept springing out of the ground and knew I had to do something fast! The tandem saurus unit idea wasn't going to work, there just wasn't time to find a gap in the line or go around. The saurus cavalry boldly charged Klaus' smaller unit of zombies hoping to smash through and circle back to support the warriors.

Meanwhile the skinks moved down from the hill, getting out of the line of sight of the fel bats and zombies but still allowing some javelins to be thrown. Itza Otltza killed a bat with forked lightning.

The saurus cavalry managed to kill a good amount of zombies, winning combat but Klaus was unscaved after the initial combat round. Matt soon bought most of the lost zombies back in his turn.

Turn 4

Remember I said the river did actually play a part in the battle? Remembering that my skinks are aquatic and can move through water unhindered, they did exactly that! Sadly they have to double time and so couldn't shoot but I was confident I could run rings around the zombies and maybe even win in combat against the bats. I always had magic too.

On the other side of the action, Matt lined up his skeletons and Wight Lord ready to get a nasty rear charge into my saurus cavalry with Vanhel's Danse Macabre. I threw all of my dice to stop it and did. This left Matt able to raise and raise again for this turn but it wasn't enough to save Klaus and his zombie unit as the saurus cavalry killed him and the last zombie.

In Matt's turn he charged the now unengaged saurus cavalry and I elected to flee, easily getting away. Zviko, seeing the coming combat, left the unit to hide behind the skeletons and the saurus warriors and the now substantial zombie block faced each other, blowing kisses.

Turn 5

I elected to charge the zombie unit with my saurus warriors! Oops, it was an inch short, so I shuffled forward 4". The saurus cavalry rallied.

Seeing an opportunity to actually throw some javelins rather than just run around, the skinks moved around the zombies to a more central position and did just that, killing... 1! I had also seen an opportunity to potently fry a certain lone undead general.

In Matt's turn, he counter charged my floundering saurus warriors and manouevered his skeletons and Wight Lord to join in next turn. The cat & mouse game of the zombies and skinks carried on as his zombies turn to face and the bats moved behind the saurus warriors.

Combat went very well for me, I managed to kill enough zombies to win and didn't lose any warriors.

Turn 6

Lizardmen turn 6, my saurus cavalry moved up. Too little too late but they had some kills under their belt. The skinks moved closer up the field and threw some javelins at the bats, killing one.

Itza Otltza cast forked lightning on Zviko! I got irresistible force and rolled 4 hits! 3 wounded!

Matt saved all but one with his ward save! Zviko only took one wound.

In combat my saurus warriors killed off the last remaining zombies and became unengaged... briefly. There was nothing they could do about Matt's charge in their flank with the skeletons, Wight Lord and Zviko who had magically healed himself of his wound.

The skinks also came a cropper, charged from one direction by the remaining bat and the other by the zombies, they failed their fear test and got run down before combat even began.

In combat, Matt challenged and I accepted thinking "what's the worst he could do, Kai Gor Zlat is pretty good in combat!".

Yeah, 5+ killing blow thanks to the Sword of Kings. The game ended with my general dead and my saurus warriors also being run down.

Battle results

Surprisingly, and largely because I had 300 extra victory points for my two carnage tokens (killing zombies with saurus cavalry and warriors), the result was a minor victory to the lizardmen with 167 points being the final difference.

It didn't feel like a victory with my 5 saurus cavalry the only ones left alive and my general being reanimated as we speak, but I'll take it.

That leaves us at one a piece in our slow grow, not that either of us are counting...
